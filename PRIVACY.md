# Privacy

This standalone extension has no developer data collection endpoint, analytics, hosted service or payment integration.

When you explicitly press Send via alias, the extension transmits supported sender, recipient, subject, body, headers and captured attachment fields directly to your selected provider (Resend or Cloudflare) using your credentials. The provider processes these under its own terms and privacy policy.

Settings and credentials remain in Chrome local extension storage. The extension reads compose fields to prepare your requested send; it does not upload inbox contents. Local diagnostic summaries contain counts, timestamps, error codes and a masked sender, including its domain. Clear them in Diagnostics. Clear credentials in Delivery or uninstall the extension to remove its local storage.

Opening provider websites or this repository is optional and governed by those sites. Nothing is submitted to GitHub automatically. Do not post private correspondence or credentials in public issues.
